Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at dreamwardsupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a couple of days. You can also send a note from inside the app: Me → Scuttlebutt.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DREAMWARD. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal or the end of a free trial.

How do I get a refund?
Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. Where are my dreams?
Sign in with the same email (Me → Back up & sync), then tap Restore purchases on the plans page.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Me → Ship's settings → Abandon ship → Delete account.

Are the AI's prices exact?
No. They're estimates to help you plan. Always check real prices before you book.
