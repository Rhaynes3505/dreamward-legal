Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

DREAMWARD ("the app", "we") is made by Rhett Haynes, Arizona, USA. We don't sell your data or show ads. Most of what you make stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your dreams, plans, savings and notes, unless you turn on back up & sync.
  • How photos get sorted. To file a photo into the right Logbook chapter, the app reads its date and location on your phone.
  • Your settings, like sounds and your home port.

When the AI looks up real prices or seasons, it may search the web for the place you named. We don't send your name or email with AI requests. The app asks your permission before anything goes to the AI, and you can turn it off any time in Me → Ship's settings → AI first mate. When you share a link, our server opens that page to read its preview (title, caption and picture). To find a cover picture for a dream, our server sends only the dream's name or place to Unsplash, Pexels or Wikipedia, never anything about you. Place search suggestions come from OpenStreetMap (Photon); only the words you type in a place box are sent.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to set your home port and sort dreams by distance. You can say no and type a home port instead.

The companies we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, backups, feedback and usage.
  • Anthropic runs the AI first mate. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, our AI requests aren't used to train their models.
  • RevenueCat and Apple handle subscriptions and payments. We never see your card details.
  • Apple Maps draws the map, and OpenStreetMap powers place search.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Me → Abandon ship → Delete account). That removes your account and backed-up dreams and photos from our servers within 30 days. Deleted dreams sit in Recently deleted for 30 days on your phone, then they're gone. To ask for a copy or deletion of anything else, email dreamwardsupport@gmail.com.

Children

DREAMWARD isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention big changes in the app. Questions: dreamwardsupport@gmail.com.
